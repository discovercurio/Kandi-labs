About Kandi-Labs

It’s more than a trinket.

Some of the best memories from a festival come from the smallest things. A sprout from a stranger, a piece of kandi traded during your favorite set, or a little collectible that reminds you of an unforgettable weekend.

Kandi-Labs was created to bring more of those moments to life.

We design and 3D print creative accessories and collectibles inspired by the music, people, artists, and experiences that make rave culture so special. Whether you’re looking for something to trade, something to wear, or something to remember the experience by, we’re here to create it.

Meet the mascot

Say hi to Orbit.

Orbit is a tiny planet that sprouted somewhere between the main stage and the campgrounds. Nobody knows exactly where, and Orbit doesn’t think it matters.

Curious about everything and almost always smiling, Orbit drifts from set to set swapping kandi, handing out sprouts, and turning strangers on the rail into friends by the last song. Orbit can’t dance very well yet, but has never once let that stop the fun.

You’ll spot Orbit tucked into our designs, packaging and side quests. Keep an eye out.
